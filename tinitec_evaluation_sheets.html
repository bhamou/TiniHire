Systems Thinking
Architecture & Design Judgment
Give a system prompt (e.g. "design a notifications service for 100K users"). Do they ask clarifying questions before proposing a solution, or jump straight in?
×25%
Trade-off Reasoning
Can they articulate the cost of their decisions? SQL vs NoSQL, monolith vs microservices — do they default or think through context each time?

Technical Depth
Depth in Core Domain
Do they know the "why" behind their tools, not just the "how"? Ask them to explain one technology they use as if to a junior dev — clarity reveals mastery.
×20%
Debugging & Problem Decomposition
Present a vague bug ("app is slow in production"). Watch how they decompose. Do they isolate variables systematically, or guess randomly and hope?
×15%
Standards & Velocity
Code Quality Philosophy
What does "good code" mean to them? Do they mention maintainability, testing, readability — or just "it works and it ships"?
×10%
Velocity vs. Quality Balance
Can they ship fast without crippling debt? Have they navigated a real "cut corners now, pay later" decision? What did they choose, and what happened?
